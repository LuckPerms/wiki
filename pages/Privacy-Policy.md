This policy explains how data is handled when you use features of the LuckPerms plugin/mod (the "**client**") that upload content to our user content services, and when you view or edit that content using the LuckPerms web applications at [luckperms.net](https://luckperms.net/) (the "**web apps**", including the web editor, verbose viewer and tree viewer). The storage service at `usercontent.luckperms.net`, the real-time relay at `usersockets.luckperms.net`, the client and the web apps are together referred to as the "**service**".

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to the operators of the LuckPerms project and service ([github.com/LuckPerms](https://github.com/LuckPerms)).

## 1. What is uploaded

Depending on the command used, an upload ("**user content**") may contain:

* **Web editor sessions** (`/lp editor`, `/lp user ... editor`, `/lp group ... editor`): the permission holders selected for editing (groups, and in some cases up to 500 users), including each holder's **username and UUID**, every permission, prefix, suffix, meta, group-membership and other node assigned to them (with contexts, expiry times and values), track definitions, the list of permissions known to the server, the contexts available on the server, and metadata such as the plugin version, platform, time and command alias used.
* **Verbose logs** (`/lp verbose ... --upload`): a record of permission checks made during the recording period, including the **name and UUID of the player or entity checked**, the permission checked and its result, the contexts at the time, the thread and a stack trace (which can reveal plugin and class names) for each check, plus the filter used and the duration of the recording.
* **Permission trees** (`/lp tree`): the tree of known permissions, optionally with the results of permission checks for a chosen user (including their **username and UUID**).
* **Data exports** (`/lp export --upload`): the data in your LuckPerms storage that you select for export, which can include all groups, tracks and users, with each user's **UUID, username, primary group and every permission node** assigned to them.
* **Edits applied from the web editor** (`/lp applyedits`): the changes you make in the web editor are uploaded so that the plugin can retrieve and apply them.
* **Creator information**: the **name and UUID of the user who ran the command** (or the console), and the server platform and plugin version.

LuckPerms never uploads anything automatically. Data is uploaded only when a user runs a command that explicitly does so (for example `/lp editor`, `/lp verbose ... --upload`, `/lp tree` or `/lp export`).

LuckPerms does not intentionally upload passwords, chat messages, player IP addresses, database credentials or your configuration file. However, permission nodes, groups, meta values, display names and contexts are free-form text defined by server administrators and plugins, and may contain information you consider sensitive. **Please review what you upload, and only share content you are comfortable making available to anyone who has the link.**

### Player data

Uploads can contain the usernames and UUIDs of your players, together with the permissions and ranks assigned to them. This is personal data. If you upload content, you are responsible for ensuring you are entitled to do so (see section 9).

### Real-time communication (web editor)

The web editor uses a WebSocket relay to communicate between your server and your browser so that changes can be sent back to the server. Messages sent via the relay are cryptographically signed using a key pair generated by your server, and the server only accepts changes from editor sessions it has trusted. The relay forwards messages between participants and does not permanently store them. A list of trusted editor keys is kept on **your own** server in its configuration directory, not by us.

## 2. Visibility of uploads

Each upload is assigned a randomly generated identifier, which forms part of its link. Uploads are not listed or publicly indexed by us, but **anyone who has the link can view and download the content**. Web editor links in particular allow people with the link to view, and (if your server trusts the session) apply permission changes, so treat them as sensitive and share them only with people you trust.

## 3. How we use the data

User content is processed only to:

* store it and make it available to people who have the link via the web apps and the raw content endpoint;
* relay web editor messages between your server and your browser;
* operate, secure, maintain and improve the service (including preventing abuse and diagnosing faults); and
* produce aggregate, non-identifying statistics about use of the service.

We do not sell user content, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of individuals. We do not share it with third parties other than the service providers described below, who process it on our behalf.

## 4. Other data we process

* **Request logs.** When you use the storage service, relay or web apps, our servers and hosting providers may record technical information such as your IP address, user agent, the URL requested, content size and timestamps. This is used for security, rate-limiting, abuse prevention, and debugging.
* **Analytics.** The web apps use a privacy-focused, cookie-less analytics tool to measure aggregate page views. It does not use cookies or build personal profiles.
* **Caching.** Content may be cached briefly by the storage service and by content delivery networks and your browser to improve performance.
* **Translations.** The client may download translation files from our servers. This does not involve uploading any user content.

## 5. Service providers and international transfers

We use third-party service providers to operate the service. They process data only on our behalf, and only for the purposes described in this policy. The categories of provider are:

* **Server hosting:** for the storage service and real-time relay, including stored uploads and request logs. Our servers are located in Germany.
* **Object storage:** for larger or longer-lived uploads. This storage is located in the Netherlands.
* **Content delivery, DNS and security:** requests to the service (including your IP address and uploaded content in transit) pass through a global content delivery and DDoS protection network.
* **Website hosting:** for the web apps, viewer and documentation.

Some of these providers operate globally, so your data (including your IP address and any content you upload or view) may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as an adequacy decision, data privacy framework certification or standard contractual clauses. You can contact us (see section 15) for more information about the providers we use.

## 6. Retention

Uploaded content is stored for a limited time and then automatically and permanently deleted. At the time of writing:

| Type of upload                                                        | Retention |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Verbose logs, permission trees, data exports and other client uploads | 14 days   |
| Web editor sessions and related real-time data                        | 1 hour    |

Individual uploads may not exceed 10 MB. These periods and limits may change, and are not a guarantee. **The service is not intended for permanent storage**; if you need to keep a copy of your data, use the local (file-based) options of the relevant commands, such as `/lp export` without uploading.

Request logs are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in section 4.

We cannot retrieve or delete copies that have already been downloaded, cached or shared by others.

## 7. Legal basis (UK/EEA users)

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we process personal data on the basis of our **legitimate interests** in operating, securing and maintaining a permissions-management service, and, in the case of content you choose to upload, to provide the service you have requested. We consider that this processing is proportionate and has minimal impact on your privacy.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you; to object to or request restriction of processing; and to complain to your local data protection authority.

To request removal of an upload, please contact us using the details in section 15. We may need to verify that you are entitled to make the request. Because uploads are identified only by their link, we may be unable to locate content without it. If you are a player whose username or UUID appears in an upload, please contact the administrator of the relevant server in the first instance.

## 9. Your responsibilities

If you upload content that includes information about other people (such as the usernames, UUIDs and permissions of your players), you are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis to share it, and that doing so complies with applicable laws (such as data protection laws) and the privacy expectations of your community. Where appropriate, you should mention this in your own server's privacy policy. Do not upload content that you do not have the right to share or that is unlawful.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the service, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), signed web editor messages and rate-limiting. However, no system is completely secure, and because anyone with a link can access an upload, we cannot guarantee the confidentiality of user content. Do not share web editor links publicly.

## 11. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them directly. Server administrators may, however, upload data about their players. If you believe a child's personal data has been uploaded, please contact us so that it can be removed.

## 12. Self-hosting and third-party instances

The storage, relay and web app URLs used by the client can be changed in its configuration file. If you use a self-hosted or third-party instance, **that instance's operator, not us, is responsible for your data**, and this policy does not apply to it.
